# 📘 50-Minute Lesson Plan
## Designing Adaptive English Lessons with Customized Apps

---

# 👥 1. Learner Profile

- Grade: High School Grade 1
- English Level: Lower-intermediate to intermediate

### 🔍 Learner Needs
- Difficulty understanding vocabulary in reading passages
- Low confidence in reading English texts
- Limited knowledge of phrasal verbs, collocations, and word relationships
- Need for repeated and meaningful vocabulary exposure
- Need for listening support during reading activities

---

# 🏫 2. Class Setting

- Context: Korean high school English classroom
- Class Length: 50 minutes

### 💻 Classroom Environment
- Computer classroom or students’ smartphones/tablets
- Streamlit web app connected through QR code or link

### 🤝 Learning Format
- Individual learning
- Pair discussion
- Whole-class feedback

---

# 📖 3. Lesson Topic

## Improving English Reading and Listening Through Vocabulary Knowledge, Language Chunks, and TTS-Supported Reading

---

# 🎯 4. Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

1. understand key vocabulary and language chunks related to reading
2. identify meanings of phrasal verbs and collocations in context
3. infer word meanings through etymology and word relationships
4. improve reading comprehension through repeated vocabulary exposure
5. improve listening comprehension through TTS-supported reading
6. actively participate in app-based English learning activities

---

# 🧩 5. Target Language

- Etymology (prefixes and roots)
- Phrasal verbs
- Collocations
- Idioms and proverbs
- Synonyms and antonyms
- Polysemous words

### ✨ Examples
- give up
- pay attention
- break down
- look after
- active / inactive
- bright (light / smart)

---

# 📝 6. Teaching Procedure

| Stage | Duration | Teacher Activities | Student Activities | Streamlit App Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 5 min | Introduce lesson topic and explain app use | Access app using QR code | APP_Smart_Class_Tools |
| Vocabulary Learning | 10 min | Explain prefixes and word meanings | Explore etymology section | APP_Etymology |
| Guided Practice | 10 min | Model phrasal verb and collocation use | Complete matching and meaning tasks | APP_Phrasal_Verbs / APP_Collocations |
| Textbook Reading with TTS | 10 min | Present textbook reading passage and TTS audio | Read while listening to TTS-supported text | APP_Textbook_Reading |
| Practice Check Quiz | 10 min | Monitor and provide feedback | Complete quizzes and review errors | All apps |
| Reflection & Wrap-up | 5 min | Summarize lesson and assign review | Share difficult expressions and reflections | Review sections |

---

# 👨‍🎓 7. Student Activities

Students will:

- explore vocabulary patterns through prefixes and roots
- complete app-based quizzes
- identify language chunks in reading passages
- listen to TTS-supported reading passages
- practice repeated reading and listening
- discuss meanings with partners
- review incorrect answers
- revisit expressions through repeated practice

---

# 👩‍🏫 8. Teacher Role

The teacher will:

- guide students through app activities
- provide scaffolding and explanations
- monitor learner progress
- support struggling learners
- encourage interaction and reflection
- connect vocabulary learning to reading comprehension and listening fluency

---

# 📱 9. App Integration

The customized Streamlit app supports adaptive learning by:

- allowing students to learn at their own pace
- providing immediate feedback
- offering repeated review opportunities
- organizing learning into manageable sections
- supporting noticing, practice, use, and revisiting
- integrating reading and listening through TTS functions
- increasing learner engagement through interactive quizzes and multimodal input

## 🛠 Apps Used
- APP_Etymology.py
- APP_Phrasal_Verbs.py
- APP_Collocations.py
- APP_Idioms_Proverbs.py
- APP_Word_Relations.py
- APP_Textbook_Reading.py

---

# 📊 10. Feedback and Assessment

## ✅ Formative Assessment
- quiz scores
- participation in app activities
- pair discussion
- teacher observation

## 💬 Feedback
- immediate app-based feedback
- teacher clarification and scaffolding
- review of incorrect answers

---

# 🌱 11. Expected Learning Outcomes

Students are expected to:

- improve vocabulary knowledge needed for reading
- recognize common language chunks more quickly
- improve reading and listening fluency
- develop confidence in reading English
- become more engaged in English learning
- use digital tools meaningfully for language learning

The lesson is expected to support gradual vocabulary development through repeated exposure, meaningful practice, TTS-supported reading, and learner-centered interaction.
